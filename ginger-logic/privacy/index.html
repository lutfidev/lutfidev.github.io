Privacy Policy

Ginger Logic — com.gingerlogic.app

Effective November 3, 2026 · Last updated November 3, 2026

Ginger Logic is a puzzle game that runs entirely on your device. There is no sign-in, no account, and no server of ours, so we never receive your data. This page explains what stays on your phone and the only data that leaves it: the data Google AdMob processes to show optional ads.

Summary
  • We (the developer) do not collect, store, or receive your personal data.
  • Your progress (stars, coins, Oyen’s room, settings) is stored locally on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device’s Advertising ID.
  • Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland), ads are personalized only with your consent, and you can change that choice in the game at any time.
  • No camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage permissions.

1. Data we collect

None. The game has no login and no backend, and makes no network requests of its own; the only network traffic comes from the ads SDK described in the third-party services section. The developer does not receive your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other identity information.

2. Local storage on your device

Game data is saved in the app’s private storage on your device and is never sent to us. It includes:

  • Stars, coins, and unlocked hints for each level.
  • Oyen’s room: placed items, adopted cats, accessories, snacks, and how full and happy each cat is.
  • The daily bonus and tutorial progress.
  • Settings: language, music, and sound effects.

How to delete it: use Settings › Reset progress in the game, clear the app’s data via Settings › Apps › Ginger Logic › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app. Because we keep no copy on any server, there is nothing left that you need to ask us to delete.

Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account (Android Auto Backup), so your progress can return when you switch phones or reinstall. That backup is handled by Google under its own privacy policy; we cannot read it. You can turn backup off in your phone’s settings.

3. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, for example to unlock a hint or earn extra coins. An ad only plays when you choose to watch one; there are no banner or interstitial ads. Ad content is limited to the “G” (general audiences) rating.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following data from your device:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID and app set IDAd delivery and personalization (where allowed), frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention.
IP addressEstimating your general location (city or country level) and fraud prevention.
Device and app informationDevice model, OS version, and language, for compatible ads and diagnostics.
Ad interactionsTaps and video views, for reporting and measurement.
DiagnosticsPerformance and crash information of the ads SDK.

This data is sent directly to Google, encrypted in transit. The developer does not receive and cannot access it; we only see aggregate reports (such as impression counts and revenue) in the AdMob dashboard.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Ad consent and the Advertising ID

In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. If you do not consent, Google shows non-personalized or limited ads, or none. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Ad consent settings (shown where consent is required).

The game declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission, which Google Play requires for apps that show ads. The Advertising ID is a user-resettable identifier, not a permanent device identifier.

  • Settings › Google › All services › Ads on your phone: choose Delete advertising ID to remove it, or Reset advertising ID to start fresh.
  • Turning off ad personalization does not remove ads, but they will no longer be based on your interests.

5. App permissions

Ginger Logic itself requests no permissions. The ads SDK and the Android libraries it depends on add the following:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: load ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: see the Advertising ID section above.
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*: Android Privacy Sandbox APIs for ad relevance and measurement.
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE: short background tasks run by these libraries.

The game does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, SMS, phone, or file/storage access.

6. Children

Ginger Logic is a casual game for a general audience aged 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and want to ask about their data, contact us and we will help escalate it to Google.

7. Data security

Because we do not transmit or store any data on a server, there is no database of ours that could be breached. Game data lives in the app’s private storage, which under Android’s security model only this app can read.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data. Because we hold none, a deletion request to us is unnecessary: reset your progress in the game, clear the app’s data, or uninstall it (see the local storage section). For data held by Google through AdMob, use My Ad Center and Google Account data & privacy.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date at the top.
