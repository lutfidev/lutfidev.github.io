Privacy Policy

Warung Tycoon — com.warungtycoon.app

Effective August 5, 2026 · Last updated August 5, 2026

Warung Tycoon is an idle game that runs entirely on your device. We have no server, no user accounts, and we do not collect any personal data. This page explains the only data that leaves your device — the data processed by Google AdMob to serve ads.

Summary
  • We (the developer) do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data anywhere.
  • All game progress is stored locally on your device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device’s Advertising ID.
  • There is no login and no request for camera, location, contacts, microphone, or storage permissions.

1. Data we collect

None. This app has no backend, no login system, and makes no network requests other than those made by the third-party SDKs described in the third-party services section. The developer does not receive your name, email address, phone number, location, contact list, photos, or any form of identity information.

2. Local storage on your device

Game progress is stored in the app’s internal storage using a local database (Hive). This data never leaves your device. It includes:

  • In-game currency, product levels, and manager status.
  • Loyal customer count and prestige (“Open Branch”) history.
  • Game statistics such as total lifetime earnings.
  • The last save timestamp, used to calculate offline earnings.
  • In-app purchase status (e.g. whether “Remove Ads” has been purchased).

How to delete it: clear the app’s data via Settings › Apps › Warung Tycoon › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app. Either action permanently deletes all local data. Because we do not keep a copy on any server, there is no remaining data you need to ask us to delete.

If Android Auto Backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may copy app data to your Google Drive account. This process is managed entirely by Android and Google, not by us, and is subject to Google’s privacy policy.

3. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

This app shows optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads via Google AdMob, a service owned by Google LLC. Watching an ad is always optional and your choice; interstitial ads only appear after an “Open Branch” action.

To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process the following data from your device:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID)Identifies the device for ad personalization, frequency capping, and ad fraud detection.
IP addressCoarse location estimation (typically city/country level) and relevant ad selection.
Device informationDevice model, OS version, language, and carrier — for compatibility and ad targeting.
Ad interactionsImpressions, clicks, and video completions, for reporting and payment.

This data is collected directly by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and sent to Google. The Warung Tycoon developer does not receive and cannot access this raw data — we only see aggregate reports (impression counts and revenue) in the AdMob dashboard.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from partner sites and apps · AdMob Policy

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

The app offers two one-time purchases: “Remove Ads + Bonus” and “Auto Manager”. All transactions are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive, or store card numbers, bank details, billing addresses, or your identity. The app only receives a “purchased” or “not purchased” status back from Google Play, which is then stored locally.

4. Advertising ID

This app declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission. Google Play requires this permission for apps that show ads on Android 13 (API 33) and above. The Advertising ID is a user-resettable identifier, not a permanent device identifier.

Your controls:

  • Settings › Google › All services › Ads — choose Delete advertising ID to remove it, or Reset advertising ID to start fresh.
  • Turning off ad personalization does not remove ads, but the ads shown will no longer be based on your interests.
  • Purchasing “Remove Ads” permanently stops all ad serving in this app.

This app is distributed for users in Indonesia. It does not yet include the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form required for users in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, so those regions are excluded from distribution.

5. App permissions

The app only requests the following permissions, all for ads and purchases:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — load ads and verify purchases.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — see the Advertising ID section above.
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* — Android Privacy Sandbox APIs for ad relevance and measurement.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — in-app purchases.

The app does not request camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, SMS, phone, or external file/storage access permissions.

6. Children

Warung Tycoon is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. This app is not enrolled in Google Play’s “Designed for Families” program. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child’s data has been collected through one of the third-party services above, please contact us and we will help escalate it to Google.

7. Data security

Because we do not transmit or store any data on a server, there is no database of ours that could be breached. Game data is stored in the app’s internal storage which, under Android’s security model, is only accessible to this app itself.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data. Because we do not store any personal data, a deletion request to us is unnecessary — simply clear the app’s data or uninstall it (see the local storage section). For data held by Google through AdMob, use the controls at My Ad Center and Google Account data & privacy.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top of the page will be revised. Material changes will be announced via Google Play release notes before they take effect.
