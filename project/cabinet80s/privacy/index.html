Privacy Policy

Cabinet80s: Offline Retro Game — com.cabinet80s.app

Last updated September 19, 2026

Cabinet80s does not collect any data about you. It has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no accounts and no network access. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because the app cannot transmit anything.

Summary
  • We (the developer) do not collect, store, or transmit any of your data.
  • There is no account, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • The app declares no Android permissions at all, including internet access.
  • Your settings and scores are stored locally and deleted when you uninstall the app.

1. Data we collect

Nothing. Cabinet80s:

  • does not collect or transmit personal information;
  • does not contain advertising or read advertising identifiers;
  • does not use analytics or crash-reporting services;
  • does not require an account, an email address or a sign-in;
  • does not contain in-app purchases or subscriptions.

2. What is stored on your device

Cabinet80s saves a small amount of information on the phone itself, so the app remembers where you left off:

  • Your settings, such as whether CRT scanlines, sound, haptics and the high-contrast theme are on.
  • Your best score for each cabinet, and which cabinets you have played.
  • Which cabinet files you have opened, which is how trophies are awarded.

This is written to the app’s own private storage using Android’s standard preferences. It never leaves the device, it is not readable by other apps, and nobody — including the developer — can see it.

3. App permissions

Cabinet80s does not request any Android permission. The installed app declares none at all — no internet access, no location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone and no storage access.

4. Leaderboards

The leaderboard shown inside a cabinet file is fictional sample content included with the app. It is not a real ranking, it is not shared with anyone, and no score of yours is uploaded to it.

5. Payment

Cabinet80s is a paid app, bought once on Google Play before it is downloaded. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play under Google’s privacy policy, and the app itself never sees, requests or stores any payment information.

6. Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Cabinet80s deletes everything it stored on the device.
  • Because we never receive your data, there is nothing to request, export or erase from a server.

7. Children

Cabinet80s is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a permission, a network call or a third-party SDK, this policy will be updated before that version ships. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised with every change.
