Privacy Policy

Tropimori: Cozy Island Farm — com.tropimori.app

Effective November 20, 2026 · Last updated November 20, 2026

Tropimori is a cozy farming game for Android, intended for players aged 13 and over. The game has no account system and no developer server. This page explains the data the game stores on your device and the Google services that process data in the production version of the app.

Summary
  • Game progress is stored locally on your device. There is no login and no cloud sync.
  • Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob and are shown only when you choose an ad button.
  • Crashes are reported through Firebase Crashlytics. Google Analytics is not installed.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card number or Google account password.
  • The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or GPS location access.

1. Game progress and preferences

The game saves the following on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Garden, home, and island progress, inventory, coins, level, and orders.
  • Settings such as language, sound, and notification preferences.
  • Activity timestamps, used so crops keep growing while the game is closed.
  • Purchase entitlement records (for example, whether “Remove Ads” was bought) and local transaction markers that prevent duplicate delivery.
  • A random player ID created on first launch, shown in Settings.

This progress is not sent to any developer server. How to delete it: clear the app's data via Settings › Apps › Tropimori › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app. Clearing data deletes local progress and does not restore consumable packs that were already used.

If Android backup or device transfer is enabled, the operating system may copy app data. That process is managed by Android and Google according to your device settings.

2. Third-party services

Google AdMob (player-initiated rewarded ads)

The game uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads. A video is shown only after you choose an ad button; closing a video early does not earn a reward. Once the SDK's privacy requirements permit it, an ad may be preloaded before you choose that button. “Remove Ads” skips rewarded videos while the normal in-game activity limits still apply.

The game requests non-personalized ads with teen age treatment. This does not stop all ad-related data processing. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process:

DataPurpose
IP address and approximate network-based locationAd delivery and regional compliance.
Device identifiers (including the Advertising ID)Frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention.
Ad interactionsImpressions, clicks, and video completions for reporting.
DiagnosticsSDK performance and abuse prevention.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland), a privacy consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears. You can reopen the available choices at any time in Settings › Ad privacy inside the game.

The developer only sees aggregate reports (impressions and revenue) in the AdMob dashboard and does not receive this raw data.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

The production version uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect crashes and improve stability. Crashlytics processes crash reports, device and app information, and an installation identifier. The game's own error reports are limited to the error type and stack trace. We do not attach garden progress, player IDs, or purchase tokens to reports, and we do not install Google Analytics. Diagnostics that were already sent are not deleted automatically when you uninstall the app.

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Garden packs and “Remove Ads” are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment and your payment account. The game only receives the product and transaction status needed to deliver items, handle pending purchases, and restore “Remove Ads”. Consumable packs that were already used cannot be recovered through “Restore purchases”.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information in ads · Firebase privacy

3. Notifications

Harvest and cooking reminders are scheduled locally on your device, only if you turn them on and grant notification permission. No push service is used for these reminders. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android settings.

4. App permissions

  • INTERNET — load ads, process purchases, and send crash reports.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show the optional harvest and cooking reminders.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — keep scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Permissions added by Google's SDKs, such as com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and com.android.vending.BILLING, for ads and purchases.

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, phone, or GPS location access.

5. Service providers and retention

AdMob, Google Play, and Firebase process data according to their own configuration and Google's policies, and processing may take place outside your country. Local progress remains as long as the app's data is kept on your device; data held by Google services follows the provider's terms. We do not sell your data or copies of your game progress.

6. Children

Tropimori is not directed at children under 13 and is not enrolled in Google Play's “Designed for Families” program. If you become aware of use or data concerning a child under 13 associated with this game, please contact us.

7. Your choices and requests

You can change the available ad privacy choices, manage notifications, clear local app data through Android, or contact us with questions about data. Requests about data managed by Google services may need that provider's own process; we will explain the available steps when you contact us. You can also use My Ad Center and Google Account data & privacy.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices or services change, this policy will be updated and the “Last updated” date at the top will be revised.
