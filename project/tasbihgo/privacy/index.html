Privacy Policy

TasbihGo - Tasbih & Dzikir — com.tasbihgo.app

Last updated October 3, 2026

TasbihGo keeps your dhikr counts, history, statistics and settings on your device. A few features talk to online services — the leaderboard, Premium, and the app update check — and this page explains exactly what each of them sends.

Summary
  • Your counters, dhikr history, statistics, reminders and profile are stored locally, and Android Auto Backup is switched off.
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service.
  • The public leaderboard is opt-in and off by default. Only a display name, an optional Instagram username, your streak and your total count are shared, and you can remove them at any time.
  • Your location is used on the device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never sent to our server.
  • Premium is billed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. What is stored on your device

  • Counter progress for each dhikr, your custom dhikr presets and dhikr chains.
  • Your dhikr history, which powers statistics, streaks and weekly targets.
  • Bookmarked adhkar and duas.
  • Your profile: the name and optional email address you enter, and your avatar. A photo you pick as an avatar is copied into the app’s private storage.
  • Your last known location (latitude and longitude), kept for up to 24 hours so prayer times load without asking for a new position each time.
  • Your leaderboard display name and optional Instagram username.
  • Reminder times, language, theme, sound and vibration preferences, and home-screen widget state.

All of it lives in a local database and Android preferences in the app’s private storage. Under Android’s security model, other apps cannot read it. Your profile name and email are never sent anywhere.

2. Location

With your permission, TasbihGo reads your approximate location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Both calculations run entirely on your device. To show the name of your city, the coordinates are passed to Android’s built-in geocoding service, which on most phones is provided by Google under Google’s privacy policy. Your location is never sent to our server and is never part of the leaderboard.

TasbihGo does not request contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or call-log access.

5. Premium

Premium is an optional subscription sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the app and RevenueCat only learn whether your subscription is active. We never see, receive or store card numbers, bank details or billing addresses. You can cancel at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions.

6. Backups and exports you create

Create Backup writes a JSON file containing your app data and lets you save it or share it with an app you choose. Restore reads a backup file you pick. Export Statistics (Premium) produces a PDF, CSV or Excel file of your dhikr statistics and hands it to the app you choose. What happens to those copies afterwards is governed by the app you sent them to. TasbihGo does not upload them anywhere.

Android’s own Auto Backup is switched off: the app declares android:allowBackup="false", so your data is not copied to your Google Drive backup.

7. Feedback and bug reports

The Feedback and Report Bug screens open your email app or WhatsApp with a message pre-filled for you. A bug report includes your device brand and model, Android version and app version so the problem can be reproduced. Nothing is sent until you send that message yourself, and you can edit or delete any part of it first.

8. Deleting your data

  • Turning off leaderboard participation immediately deletes your row from the leaderboard server.
  • More › Delete Data permanently deletes your counters, dhikr presets, chains, statistics, bookmarks and profile (name, email and avatar) from the device, withdraws you from the leaderboard and deletes your leaderboard row from the server. Settings and preferences are kept.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.
  • To have anything else removed from our server, such as your anonymous ID or a redeemed VIP code, email us from the address below.

9. Children

TasbihGo is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The leaderboard is off by default, and joining it shares only a display name of your choosing.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app gains a feature that sends new kinds of data, this policy will be updated before that version ships. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised with every change.
